⛩️ Japanese Culture & Etiquette

Learn Japanese workplace culture, business manners, and proper etiquette to work effectively with Japanese colleagues.

🙇

Bowing (お辞儀 - Ojigi)

Essential Japanese greeting

15° - Casual

Eshaku (会釈) - Greeting coworkers

30° - Respect

Keirei (敬礼) - Meeting clients/boss

45° - Deep

Saikeirei (最敬礼) - Apologies/gratitude

💳

Business Cards (名刺 - Meishi)

Proper exchange etiquette

  • Present with both hands
  • Text facing recipient
  • Receive with both hands
  • Study the card before putting away
  • Never write on someone's card
🤝

Meeting Etiquette (会議マナー)

Professional behavior

  • Arrive 5 minutes early
  • Let seniors speak first
  • Take notes during meeting
  • Say "失礼します" when entering/leaving
  • Don't interrupt or argue directly
💬

Communication Style (コミュニケーション)

Indirect & respectful

❌ Don't say: "No" directly ✅ Say instead: "ちょっと難しいですね" (It's a bit difficult)
❌ Don't say: "I disagree" ✅ Say instead: "別の考え方もあります" (There's another way to think about it)
⏰

Punctuality (時間厳守)

Time is sacred in Japan

  • Being late is very disrespectful
  • Arrive 5-10 minutes early always
  • Notify immediately if delayed
  • Apologize profusely if late
